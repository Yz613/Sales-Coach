# Integration brand assets

Icons are served locally so the integrations page does not depend on a third-party image service.

- Fathom, Fireflies, tl;dv, Gong, Close, Pipedrive, Attio: favicons from the respective official product websites, retrieved October 1, 2026.
- HubSpot, Make: Simple Icons SVGs (CC0), retrieved from https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons on October 1, 2026. Zapier uses its official favicon, refreshed October 2, 2026.

Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners. They identify the available connection targets.


Added October 2, 2026. Simple Icons SVGs are CC0 and use the brand color; remaining marks are served unchanged from official product sites/CDNs.
